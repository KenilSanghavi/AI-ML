  # 📊 Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard analyzes the **Sample - Superstore** dataset to provide insights into sales, profit, quantity, shipping modes, product categories, and regional performance.

The dashboard uses interactive visuals and slicers to make the analysis easy to understand and explore.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Compare sales across different product categories and sub-categories.
* Analyze sales and quantity across regions.
* Understand the distribution of different shipping modes.
* Track important business KPIs such as Sales, Profit, Quantity, and Discount.
* Provide interactive filtering using slicers.
* Create a clean and visually appealing business dashboard.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Components

### 1. KPI Cards

The dashboard contains KPI cards to provide a quick overview of important metrics:

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Profit**
* **Total Quantity**
* **Average/Overall Discount**

These KPIs help users quickly understand the overall business performance.

### 2. Sales by Category

A bar chart is used to compare sales across different product categories and sub-categories.

This helps identify which categories and products contribute the most to total sales.

### 3. Sales by Shipping Mode

The dashboard analyzes sales according to different shipping modes.

This provides an overview of customer shipping preferences and their contribution to sales.

### 4. Profit by Region

A regional analysis is included to compare profitability across different regions.

This helps identify high-performing and low-performing regions.

### 5. Quantity by Region

The dashboard visualizes the quantity of products sold across regions, helping understand regional sales volume.

### 6. Discount & Shipping Analysis

The dashboard also includes analysis of discounts in relation to shipping modes and customer segments.

This can help understand how discounting varies across different business segments.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard contains interactive slicers that allow users to filter the analysis dynamically.

### Available Filters

* **Region**
* **Order Date**
* **Category**

Users can select one or multiple values to update the dashboard visuals automatically.

---

## 🔄 Dashboard Interactivity

The dashboard uses Power BI's visual interaction features.

Selecting a value from one visual can filter or highlight information in other visuals. For example:

> Selecting a particular region can update the corresponding sales, profit, quantity, and category analysis.

This makes the dashboard more interactive and useful for exploratory analysis.
